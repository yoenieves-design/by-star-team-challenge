# BY STAR - Project Demo outline

This is English presentation content to turn into a finished deck or recording. It is not itself a finished slide deck, video or competition submission.

The November 13, 2026 Project Submission Guide requires a video of **30 seconds or less** OR a slide deck of **no more than seven slides**, counting title and introduction (supplied PDF, page 4). The app's public URL belongs with the final project; it does not replace this presentation. The recommendation for BY STAR is a seven-slide deck because it provides room to show the workflow and explain data provenance without rushing the story.

## Seven-slide deck

Use exactly the seven slides below; include credits in those slides rather than adding an eighth. All visible text must be in English. Use legible screenshots of the implemented app. The descriptions of visuals below are production directions, not additional slide text.

### 1. BY STAR - Mission Health

**Understand the reading. Check the context. Record the next action.**

Team: **by-star**

Team members: **[Insert actual registered participants]**

Visual: a clear Health screenshot with the Simulation label visible. Keep the actual team identities separate from the fictional crew in the app.

### 2. A reading needs context

**Far from Earth, a crew member needs more than a number.**

- Is this observation current and usable?
- Does it belong to rest, exercise or EVA recovery?
- What action remains, and who will help?

Visual: one health observation beside its measurement time, source and activity. Explain the need through the fictional post-EVA scenario, without asserting a diagnosis.

### 3. From observation to follow-up

**Health → Assist → recorded outcome**

1. Inspect the observation and breathing environment.
2. Follow a guided check and request crew support.
3. Record the outcome and confirm receipt.

**A completed checklist does not erase an unresolved issue.**

Visual: two readable screenshots showing evidence and an outcome or pending handoff. Show one concrete case rather than every feature.

### 4. Different sources, shared context

**Every input keeps its identity, time and quality.**

- Body: heart rate, skin temperature, movement.
- Environment: oxygen fraction, CO₂ partial pressure, pressure.
- Clinical: specimen identity, held and corrected urine reports.
- Exercise: cycle workload, cadence, independent chest pulse.

**Missing and delayed observations remain visible.**

Visual: exercise machine signal gap beside the independent body reading, or the Sensors view. Include units and timestamps in the crop.

### 5. NASA evidence and honest simulation

**Real evidence informs the design; fictional telemetry makes it reproducible.**

- NASA OSDR RadLab: 16 historical ISS dose-rate records, shown separately.
- NASA medical and interface references inform crew review and data-quality presentation.
- NASA exercise and inertial-mass references inform the workout example.

**Crew, lunar telemetry, clinical reports and procedures are synthetic.**

Visual: a small reference-panel screenshot and a separate Simulation label. Avoid a visual connection implying that historical ISS radiation records drive the lunar alerts.

Include readable source labels and hyperlinks: [RadLab](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/data-api/), [crew interfaces](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/10-0-crew-interfaces-vol-2/), [medical evaluation §6.5.1](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ochmo-std-100-1a-rev-a-signed.pdf#page=52), [exercise](https://www.nasa.gov/general/astronaut-exercise/). The full source list belongs in the project page as well.

### 6. What works now, what comes next

**Working prototype:** four pages, reproducible replay, source checks, guided reviews, clinical revisions, workout records, local persistence and optional voice controls.

**Verified implementation:** 30 unit tests and 17 browser tests passed for the released app.

**Future operational work:** authenticated device gateways, protected medical records, validated mission procedures and verified communication delivery.

**No clinical validation or live spacecraft connection is claimed.**

Visual: a compact implemented / future comparison with minimal text. Label optional browser voice accurately; it may depend on the browser vendor's internet service.

### 7. Try the mission

**Preserve the evidence. Make the next action clear.**

**Proposed value:** clearer crew follow-up when assistance is constrained, with possible future relevance to remote teams on Earth.

Visual: one clean product screenshot, readable URLs and an optional QR code tested against the public app. Credit any NASA lunar image used: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, visualization 11747.
